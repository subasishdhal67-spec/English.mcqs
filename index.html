🌼 Daffodils — William Wordsworth

1. Who wrote "Daffodils"?

Correct Answer: B. William Wordsworth

2. How does the poet compare himself at the beginning?

Correct Answer: C. Like a cloud

3. Where did the poet see the daffodils?

Correct Answer: D. Beside a lake

4. What were the daffodils doing?

Correct Answer: C. Dancing in the breeze

5. What does the poet call the daffodils?

Correct Answer: D. Golden daffodils

6. The daffodils were fluttering and dancing in the:

Correct Answer: B. Breeze

7. The daffodils were compared with the stars in the:

Correct Answer: C. Milky Way

8. About how many daffodils did the poet see?

Correct Answer: B. Ten thousand

9. What was beside the daffodils?

Correct Answer: C. The lake

10. What did the waves do beside the flowers?

Correct Answer: B. Danced

11. What did the poet feel when he saw the daffodils?

Correct Answer: C. Joy

12. What does the poet remember when he is alone?

Correct Answer: D. The daffodils

13. The memory of the flowers fills the poet's heart with:

Correct Answer: B. Pleasure

14. The poem "Daffodils" is mainly about:

Correct Answer: C. Nature and its joy

15. The daffodils are described as:

Correct Answer: B. Golden

16. What movement is repeatedly associated with the daffodils?

Correct Answer: D. Dancing

17. What natural element makes the daffodils flutter?

Correct Answer: C. Breeze

18. The poet wandered:

Correct Answer: C. Lonely as a cloud

19. What did the poet see suddenly?

Correct Answer: A. A crowd of golden daffodils

20. The daffodils stretched along the:

Correct Answer: C. Margin of a bay

21. The poet says the daffodils were continuous like:

Correct Answer: B. A never-ending line

22. What did the poet's heart do when he remembered the scene?

Correct Answer: D. Danced with the daffodils

23. What is the lasting effect of nature on the poet?

Correct Answer: C. It gives him happiness

24. "Daffodils" celebrates the beauty of:

Correct Answer: B. Nature

25. William Wordsworth is especially known as a poet of:

Correct Answer: C. Nature

🌹 The Nightingale and the Rose — Oscar Wilde

26. Who wrote "The Nightingale and the Rose"?

Correct Answer: D. Oscar Wilde

27. What did the Student need?

Correct Answer: B. A red rose

28. Why did the Student want the rose?

Correct Answer: D. To give it to the girl he loved

29. Who heard the Student's sorrow?

Correct Answer: C. The Nightingale

30. What did the Nightingale believe in?

Correct Answer: D. True love

31. What was missing from the Student's garden?

Correct Answer: D. A red rose

32. Which creature searched for a red rose?

Correct Answer: B. The Nightingale

33. What did the Nightingale sacrifice?

Correct Answer: D. Her life

34. What did the Nightingale sing about?

Correct Answer: B. Love

35. What did the Nightingale need to create the red rose?

Correct Answer: B. Her sacrifice

36. Where did the Nightingale press herself against?

Correct Answer: A. A thorn

37. What colour was the rose the Student needed?

Correct Answer: B. Red

38. What did the Student plan to give the girl?

Correct Answer: C. The red rose

39. What did the girl demand before accepting the Student?

Correct Answer: B. A red rose

40. The Nightingale represents:

Correct Answer: C. Selfless love

41. The Student represents love that is:

Correct Answer: D. Idealistic but immature

42. What happened to the red rose after the Nightingale's sacrifice?

Correct Answer: B. It bloomed

43. What did the Student do with the rose?

Correct Answer: B. Took it to the girl

44. What did the girl choose instead of the Student's rose?

Correct Answer: C. Jewels

45. What did the Student do after being rejected?

Correct Answer: B. Returned to his studies

46. What did the Student think about love at the end?

Correct Answer: B. He dismissed it as impractical

47. What is one major theme of the story?

Correct Answer: D. Love and sacrifice

48. The Nightingale mainly shows:

Correct Answer: B. The power of sacrifice

49. What does the red rose symbolize?

Correct Answer: C. Love

50. Which quality is most strongly associated with the Nightingale?

Correct Answer: C. Self-sacrifice
